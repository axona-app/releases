# Axona — Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-07-22_

We don't track you. Axona collects nothing about you: no telemetry, no analytics. Your notes are plain files on your disk, and the app doesn't send them to us.

## What the app sends

The only thing Axona sends out is an update check: it asks GitHub whether a newer version is available. That request includes your IP address, which GitHub handles as part of answering; it carries none of your notes, and we don't receive or store it.

## If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply and to keep a record of the request.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or build advertising profiles.

## As Axona grows

Some future features may send data out — for example, AI assistance (to an AI provider you choose) or optional cloud sync. Any such feature will be opt-in, and we'll update this page to explain it before it ships.
